Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Solar Panel Calculator by MagicWhale ("the App") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All calculations are performed locally on your device and never leave your computer or phone.

2. Cookies & Tracking

The App does not use cookies, analytics, or any tracking technologies. We do not track how you use the App.

3. Local Storage

The App may use your browser's local storage to remember your language preference and to enable offline functionality. This data stays on your device and is never sent to any server.

4. Third-Party Libraries

The App loads the following libraries from public CDNs for chart rendering and icons:

  • Chart.js — hosted on jsDelivr
  • Font Awesome — hosted on Cloudflare

These services may log your IP address as part of normal CDN operation. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for more details.

5. Internet Connection

After the first load, the App works fully offline. No data is sent to any server during normal use.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. It is safe for use by all ages, including in educational settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
